## About Vanguardian Financial Inc.

Vanguardian Financial Inc. was founded in 1972 by Douglas Brandon with a vision to provide professional, institutional-grade investment tools and platforms. Under the leadership of CEO Michael Keen, the company has evolved to meet the demands of modern digital asset markets while maintaining the trust, transparency, and investor-focused principles established over five decades ago.

**Mission:** To provide investors with professional-grade tools, clear financial information, and secure platforms for tracking and managing digital asset portfolios.

**Core Values:**
- Trust & Transparency
- Investor Education
- Security & Reliability
- Professional Excellence
- Innovation with Integrity
